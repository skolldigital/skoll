Frequently asked questions

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We work on redesigns, migrations and replatforming. The initial review helps decide which pages, content and URLs should carry forward.

What platforms do you build on?

We build ecommerce stores on Shopify and WooCommerce. We can also scope a custom build based on what the site and your team need.

Can I hire you to build one landing page?

Yes. A landing page or campaign microsite can be scoped as a standalone project around a specific audience, offer and conversion goal.
