What we can help with

Technical SEO audit

Review crawl and indexing coverage, redirects, canonical URLs, sitemaps and internal links. We prioritize fixes by how they affect important pages.

On-page SEO and content strategy

Map commercial searches to dedicated pages, improve titles and content, and plan resources that answer questions buyers ask before contacting you.

Local and international search

For a business with local locations, the scope can include Google Business Profile and location pages. For cross-border campaigns, we plan around the markets, languages and offers you actually serve.

Organic search measurement

Track search queries and landing-page performance, then connect those changes to inquiry or purchase data when the relevant analytics are in place.

Frequently asked questions

What does an SEO audit include?

An audit reviews how search engines discover, crawl and index your key pages. We also examine page content, internal links and search demand, then turn the findings into a prioritized roadmap.

Can you help with SEO for a website redesign?

Yes. We can review existing URLs and landing-page performance, plan redirects and internal links, and check indexing once the new site is live.

Can I start with an audit?

Yes. A standalone audit is an option when you need to understand the opportunity and decide which fixes to implement first.
