The Brief

An e-commerce brand doing Rs.2.7 Lakh in monthly revenue with 123 orders. Solid product. Decent traffic. Conversion was stuck. They needed someone to figure out why growth had plateaued — and build a system to fix it.

What We Did
01

Diagnosis first

Audit of audience, creative, funnel and product page conversion before touching a single ad.

02

Rebuilt Meta architecture

Restructured audience segmentation and rewrote the creative direction around what was actually moving people to buy.

03

Daily / weekly / monthly cadence

Daily monitoring. Weekly reallocation. Monthly compounding.

04

Outcome-led creative

Every asset measured against revenue, not vanity engagement.

The Result
Month 1: 340% sales growth · 297% increase in orders
Month 2: 160% growth on top of Month 1
Month 3: 101% further growth
Revenue: Rs.2.7L → Rs.9L/month
Orders: 123 → 392/month
Conversion rate: +157%
